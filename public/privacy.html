1. Who We Are

GoLocal ("GoLocal by NR Next") operates hyperlocal mobility, parcel, and private bus booking services in Borai, Mohanpur, Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal (PIN 721457) and nearby corridors. Data Fiduciary contact: nrnexthq@gmail.com.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account & Identity: Phone number (via Firebase Phone OTP), optional full name, and billing email.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate GPS coordinates during active booking selection, driver dispatch, live trip tracking, and active Rider foreground tracking service. Background location permission is not requested.
  • Rider Partner KYC & Compliance Documents: Voluntary Driving Licence, Vehicle RC, Commercial Insurance, Fitness, Permit, PUC, and Bus Operator permits uploaded to private Firebase Storage paths readable only by the uploader and verified GoLocal admins.
  • Safety & Communication: Optional private emergency contacts (stored isolated under your UID), active-trip messages, and SOS safety events.
  • Device Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens scoped by app surface (customer, rider, admin) solely for booking, dispatch, chat, and safety notifications.

3. How We Protect & Use Your Data

  • Zero Public Exposure of Phone Numbers: Unrelated passengers, drivers, and directory entries never receive raw phone numbers or private emergency contacts.
  • Encryption in Transit: All traffic between GoLocal Android/Web apps and Firebase/OSRM/Nominatim endpoints uses HTTPS/TLS (cleartextTrafficPermitted="false").
  • No Advertising Sales: GoLocal does not sell personal data or share location data with third-party ad networks.
  • Trip Security: Commercial rides require a 6-digit SHA-256 hashed Pickup OTP verified at pickup and a 4-digit customer Trip PIN.

4. Data Retention & Self-Service Account Deletion

You can immediately delete your GoLocal account and erase personal profile fields (name, email, phone number, photo URL, FCM tokens, and private emergency contacts) either inside the GoLocal Android app (Profile → Delete Account, typing DELETE) or on the web at GoLocal Web Account Deletion. Completed commercial GST invoices and statutory payout ledger records are retained in anonymized form only as required under Indian tax and Motor Vehicles Aggregator laws.

5. Grievance Officer & Contact

For privacy inquiries, data correction, or manual deletion support, email nrnexthq@gmail.com with your registered phone number.
