Delete Your GoLocal Account & Personal Data

You can delete your GoLocal Customer or Rider Partner account here or directly inside the GoLocal Android app (Profile → Delete Account).

What Gets Deleted Immediately

  • Your Firebase Authentication credential (uid) and active sessions.
  • Your personal profile fields on users/{uid} (name replaced with Deleted User; phone, email, and photo cleared).
  • Your customer_profiles/{uid}, emergency_contacts/{uid}, and registered FCM device tokens.
  • Your Driver Partner online availability (driver_profiles/{uid} & driver_directory/{uid} set offline immediately).

What May Be Retained for Compliance

Completed commercial GST trip invoices, commission/payout ledgers, and compliance audit entries are retained without personal contact identifiers where required by Indian tax and transport regulations. Deletion is blocked while an active trip (assigned, arriving, arrived, or in_progress) is underway.
